# Customer-Churn-Analysis
                                               

Project Overview:
This project analyzes customer churn data using Microsoft Power BI to identify customer retention patterns, understand churn behavior, and help businesses reduce customer loss.

Objectives:
1)Analyze customer churn rate and customer retention.

2)Identify customer segments with high churn rates.

3)Understand the impact of internet service types and contract types on churn.

4)Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven decisions.

Key Features:

📌 Interactive Power BI Dashboard: Visualize customer churn patterns, customer counts, churn rates, yearly charges, and support ticket metrics through interactive charts and KPIs.

📌 Customer Segmentation: Analyze customers based on internet service type, contract type, tenure, and other customer characteristics to identify groups with higher churn rates.

📌 Churn Rate Analysis: Measure and compare churn rates across different internet service categories to understand customer attrition patterns.

📌 Contract and Tenure Analysis: Examine how contract duration and customer tenure relate to churn behavior and customer retention.

📌 Customer Risk Analysis: Identify customer groups that may be at higher risk of leaving the company and support targeted retention strategies.

📌 Interactive Filtering: Use dashboard filters to explore customer churn by service type, contract, and other available attributes.


Key Insights:

📊 Customer Churn Rate: The dashboard reports approximately 27% churn among 7,043 customers, with 1,869 customers leaving the company.

📊 Contract Type and Tenure: Customers on monthly contracts and those with shorter tenure are more likely to churn than customers with longer-term contracts.

📊 Dependents and Partners: Customers without dependents or partners show a higher tendency to churn than those who have them.

📊 Gender and Age: Gender appears to have little impact on churn, while senior citizens show a lower churn tendency than non-senior customers in this analysis.

📊 Internet Service: Fiber-optic customers have a higher churn rate than customers using DSL or other internet service options.

📊 Payment Method: Electronic check users represent a prominent group among churned customers.

📊 Additional Services: Customers without services such as Tech Support, Device Protection, and Online Security tend to show greater churn risk.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI and Excel can be used to analyze customer churn, identify potential risk factors, and generate actionable insights to help businesses improve customer retention and make data-driven decisions.
